What it is deciding, and on what

Tape width comes from the package, following EIA‑481: everything up to 1210 and SOT‑23 rides 8 mm tape, SOIC‑8 and most QFNs 12 mm, wide SOICs and 7×7 QFPs 16 mm. The package itself is read out of the manufacturer part number where that number carries it, which is most passives and very few ICs — so check the picker before you print.

Standard or XL is capacity against your quantity. π(r² − r_hub²) / (carrier × pitch) with r 29.5 standard, r 42 XL, hub 7.4 mm. That reproduces the README's own figures (801 and 1678 against its ~800 and ~1600 of 0603), which is the only reason to trust it — carrier thickness is the sensitive term and it is a dropdown, not a measurement.

The label matches label(width) in container.scad exactly: a 41.4 × 12.2 × 0.8 mm flange with a 39.4 mm block on top, 3.3 mm overall — the same slot as the blanks. The file is a plain core‑spec 3MF with two objects and two base materials; assign them to two AMS slots on import. Lettering exists only in the top 3 layers, so that is the only place the filament changes. Text down puts the lettering against the build plate for a sharper colour edge, at the cost of a 1 mm overhang where the flange steps out — hidden once the card is in the slot.
